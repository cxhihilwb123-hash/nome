## Changes

- Distinguish relay-backed channels from ordinary groups in the compact chat
  list and show a `频道` badge with a channel icon.
- Give the chat header card a compact, aligned layout with one header row and
  two equal-width status chips.
- Show channel-specific copy and status: `频道消息`, `中继分发` and the public
  subscriber count. Remove the incorrect group and end-to-end-encryption copy
  from channels.
- Use `频道` rather than the misleading `安全频道` subtitle when a subscriber
  count is not available.
- Add Simplified Chinese translations for subscriber count, subscribers and
  channel owners.

## Channel member visibility

- The public subscriber count is shown in the chat toolbar and channel detail
  page when supplied by the channel summary.
- Channel owners can open the subscriber list.
- Ordinary subscribers can open the channel owner list; the complete subscriber
  list is not exposed to them.
