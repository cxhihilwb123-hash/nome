## Root cause and server repair

New channel relay handshakes produced repeated SMP proxy attempts from the
channel-relay process back through the same SMP host. The SMP container logged
`NEUnknownCAError`; ordinary groups did not use this relay path and continued
to work.

The final systemd command adds `--smp-proxy never` to
`nome-chat-relay.service`, while retaining `--smp-web-port-servers off`. This
makes the dedicated relay process connect directly to the channel owner's SMP
queue. It does not change client routing preferences.

Before mutation, the service file and both relay databases were backed up at:

`/opt/nome/backups/channel-relay-route-fix-20260801T105700Z`

An initial attempt to force the service's `--server` override hit the existing
`protocol_servers` uniqueness constraint. It was immediately rolled back from
that backup before the final `--smp-proxy never` change. Final evidence:

- `nome-chat-relay.service`: active, PID 1636905, `NRestarts=0`;
- local relay control listener: `127.0.0.1:5226`;
- public SMP and HTTPS listeners: `5223` and `443` unchanged;
- Nginx: active;
- no new relay warnings or SMP `UnknownCA` entries after the final restart;
- `https://smp.nome.im/` and `https://relay.nome.im/`: HTTP 200.
