<h1>Nome — private communication without a public user ID</h1>
<p>Nome is an end-to-end encrypted messaging app for private conversations, groups, public channels, files, and audio or video calls.</p>
<p>Nome features:</p>
<ul>
  <li>end-to-end encrypted messages, with editing, replies and deletion of messages.</li>
  <li>sending end-to-end encrypted images and files.</li>
  <li>private conversations and groups created through invitations.</li>
  <li>public channels with member reporting and moderation controls.</li>
  <li>end-to-end encrypted audio and video calls.</li>
  <li>background notifications and local profile backup and restore.</li>
</ul>
<p>Privacy design:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Nome does not require a phone number or a public user directory.</li>
  <li>Profiles, contacts, and chat history are primarily kept in the app's local data.</li>
  <li>Official routing services relay encrypted traffic so messages, files, and calls can be delivered.</li>
  <li>When activation is enabled, Nome checks installation access with the Nome activation service.</li>
</ul>
<p>Connections are created by sharing an invitation link or QR code with the intended contact. Do not share invitations with people you do not recognize.</p>
<p>Read the <a href="https://nome.im/privacy" target="_blank">Nome privacy policy</a> and <a href="https://nome.im/terms" target="_blank">terms of use</a> before using networked features.</p>
<p>For product information, downloads, privacy details, and invite applications, visit <a href="https://nome.im" target="_blank">nome.im</a>. Please use only official Nome links and do not share activation tokens or contact invitations publicly.</p>
